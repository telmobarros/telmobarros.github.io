Projects

RETAILL — AI in Retail Supply Chain Optimization 2024 - Present
LIACC · FEUP · EUREKA / ITEA · Portugal2030

European consortium project (EUREKA ITEA-certified, co-funded by Portugal2030) targeting smart supply chain management for fresh produce — fruit, vegetables, and chilled goods. Develops logistics optimization, dynamic routing, surplus-product redistribution, advanced tracking and preservation monitoring, and lifecycle assessment tools, integrating data from multiple supply chain actors and environmental sources.

Backend Routing Optimization
ERS SAMA4 — Risk-Based Healthcare Supervision 2022 - Present
LIACC · FEUP, GLINTT & ERS · Compete 2020 / ESF (POCI-05-5762-FSE-000389)

Risk-based supervision model for ERS that replaces volume-based regulatory oversight with data-driven risk profiling. Healthcare providers are assessed across seven dimensions — access, quality and safety, economic transparency, market competition, user rights, user satisfaction, and provider-regulator relationships — using standardized performance data to enable targeted regulatory intervention. Piloted in February 2023 with facilities covered by Portugal's National Health Assessment System (SINAS).

Full-stack Risk Modeling Decision Support
ERS-IA — AI for Healthcare Regulation 2020 - 2022
LIACC · FEUP & ERS · SAMA 2020 (POCI-05-5762-FSE-000205)

AI system for ERS (Portugal's independent healthcare regulator) to process and triage citizen complaints at scale. Uses NLP for automated information extraction, binary classifiers (Random Forest, SVM) for complaint categorization, and fuzzy matching for near-duplicate detection — trained on approximately 374,000 digital complaints from 2018-2020. Generates risk signals to prioritize supervisory actions and integrates with ERS's existing complaint management platform.

Full-stack Risk Modeling Decision Support ML
CIGESCOP — Intelligent Management & Control Center 2021
LIACC · FEUP & ASAE (POCI-05-5762-FSE-000215) ↗ FEUP News

Intelligent operational management and control center for ASAE, reengineering inspection workflows through AI and Data Science. Automates resource allocation, supports decision-making across operational units, and improves ASAE's interaction with citizens and businesses as part of Portugal's public administration digital transformation.

Full-stack Route Optimization Decision Support
IA.SAE — Artificial Intelligence in Food & Economic Security 2018 - 2020
LIACC · FEUP & ASAE · Funded by FCT / INCoDe.2030

AI system for ASAE (Portugal's food safety and economic authority) to optimize inspection targeting and resource allocation. Comprises five modules: intelligent risk matrix generation, consumer complaint analysis, sales-monitoring alerts, economic operator selection, and a geospatial decision-support interface for field operations.
